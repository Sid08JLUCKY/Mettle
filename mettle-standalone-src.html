Questions people ask us most.

How do I know this is not another coaching scam?+

Fair question. Two of our clients are on this page with their real names, photos and colleges, and both founders are named as well. We are a small, new company and we say so. We do not promise you a job, and on the free call we will tell you if we do not think we can help.

Do you guarantee a job?+

No. Nobody can promise you a job, and anyone who does is lying. We control your resume, your outreach and your interview practice. We do not control who an employer hires.

What does it cost?+

Four packages, all priced on this page: $199, $399, $499 and $1,199 AUD per person. You see the price before you speak to us. Discounts of up to 60% are decided on your first call, once we know where you are.

You are in India. I am looking for work in Australia. Does that work?+

Yes. Calls are on video and booked in Australian time zones, and everything we write for you follows Australian formats, including your Seek profile. We are not migration agents, so we cannot advise you on visas.

I already have a resume. Why pay for another one?+

If your resume is already getting you interviews, do not buy one from us. Take the mock interview instead, or nothing at all. The resume package is for people who never hear back.

What happens if I do the work and still get nothing?+

You tell us, and we say so publicly. We publish outcomes whether they are good or bad. A company that only shows its wins is showing you marketing, not results.
